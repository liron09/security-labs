# Case Study: Visual Anomaly Detection & Cognitive Bias

**Type:** IMINT / analytic tradecraft · **Program:** OSINT Analyst Program (Reichman University & Google)

## Task
A recruitment advertisement shows a dense image of near-identical parked cars with the line *"If you noticed this, we have found you."* Find the hidden detail and analyze the ad.

## Finding
The word **"HELP"** is written on the windshield of one car. I found it by first looking for the repeating pattern, then zooming into the cars whose headlights were on. One beam lands directly on the car with the writing.

## Analysis
- **Who:** the State Border Guard Service of Ukraine.
- **Overt message:** recruit candidates with strong observation skills for camera- and imagery-based monitoring.
- **Covert message:** self-selection. The people who can spot anomalies in noise are exactly the analysts they need.

**Cognitive biases that hide the detail:**
- *Salience bias:* the eye goes to what all the cars share, not to the exception.
- *Focus bias:* attention stays on the main subject and misses small changes.
- *Visual overload:* many near-identical items cause small anomalies to be filtered out.

**Why the slogan works:** it flips the usual dynamic. Instead of the candidate finding the job, the job "finds" the candidate, which creates a sense of personal achievement and selection.

**Intelligence values it reflects:** attention to minor detail, critical "outside the box" thinking, pattern and anomaly detection, filtering signal from noise, actively countering bias and fatigue.
