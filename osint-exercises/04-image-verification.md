# Case Study: Image Verification, a Viral Photo Out of Context

**Type:** Media verification / disinformation · **Program:** OSINT Analyst Program (Reichman University & Google)

## Task
A photo of Prince William that appears to show an offensive gesture went viral. Verify when, where and by whom it was taken, and what actually happened.

## Investigation
1. **Date and source:** traced the earliest social-media posts of the image to **23 April 2018** (the day of the royal birth announcement at St Mary's Hospital, London).
2. **Image forensics:** the image analyzed with the MeVer forensics tool (`mever.iti.gr`) showed the photographer (**Peter Nicholls / Reuters**), date, time and location in its metadata.
3. **Corroboration:** found additional frames of the same moment from other angles in Reuters' picture gallery and in news coverage (e.g. Der Spiegel).
4. **Close inspection:** zooming into the original and the other angles shows **three raised fingers**. William was signaling that he now has three children, not making an offensive gesture.

## Conclusion
The viral interpretation was false. The photo was authentic but **cropped and taken out of context**.

**Lesson:** never judge an image from a single frame or source. Verify with metadata, the original photographer/agency and additional angles of the same event.

## Tools
Reverse image search · MeVer image forensics · Reuters picture archive · news cross-referencing
